# My Favorite Series: Stranger Things


## About
**Stranger Things** is a *sci-fi horror* series on **Netflix**, created by the Duffer Brothers. It is set in the 1980s in the small town of **Hawkins, Indiana**, where a group of kids faces secret experiments and a dark dimension called the **Upside Down**.

> "Friends don't lie." – Eleven

## Why I Love It
- The 1980s setting and music
- Strong friendships between the main characters
  - The kids always support each other
  - Every character grows over the seasons
- A mix of mystery, adventure and horror
